Patch notes by mail

One mail per published patch note. Nothing is sent until you confirm, and every mail carries its own unsubscribe link.

What happens after you submit?

stepwhat happens
1The address and the consent text version are stored as pending. No mail is sent to you yet.
2A confirmation mail with a one-time link goes out in the next batch. The daily mail budget is shared with other projects of the same operator, so delivery may take several days when capacity is limited.
3Opening the link and pressing Confirm turns the subscription on. The link is valid for 48 hours. A pending request that was never confirmed is removed when the operator runs the mailer's cleanup (it runs at the start of each mail run, not on a fixed schedule), at the earliest 7 days after the request.
4Each patch-notes mail links to the patch note on this site and carries an unsubscribe link that works without signing in.

dewfpga's own tables store no IP address, browser, opens or clicks, and the mails carry no tracking pixel. The hosting of this page (GitHub Pages) and the Supabase project keep their own request logs under their own terms; this page does not control them. Newsletter mail is delivered by Resend from the operator's account. The shared stylesheet of this site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so opening the page requests them from Google.

Does this need an account?

No. The newsletter works without signing in. If you are signed in on the account page in the same tab when you confirm, the subscription is attached to that account so it shows up in the account export and goes away with the account. A subscription made without signing in stays separate; manage it with the unsubscribe link in any mail.
