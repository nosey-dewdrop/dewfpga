Why does it happen?

yosys-slang covers most of SystemVerilog and stops on the rest with this line; a real-valued parameter (localparam MAX = 1e1) is one known case, reported as yosys-crashed when yosys' own reader crashed on it.

What is the fix?

Comment out half the design until it builds, then the other half: the construct that stops it is what to rewrite or report.
