design.sv:2: ERROR [internal-tristate]: a tri-state driver (a z, at design.sv:2) on a signal that is not an inout pin of the top module: the chip has tri-state buffers on its pins only. Vivado converts such a driver to logic (UG901 Ch.5 Tristates); yosys keeps it as a $_TBUF_ cell, and nextpnr has nothing to place it on. Not a size problem. Fix: select with a mux instead: assign bus = sel ? b : a; and keep z for an inout pin only.

Why does it happen?

assign bus = sel ? a : 'z; on an internal wire describes a shared bus; a 7-series chip has no internal tri-state, Vivado rewrites it as a mux (UG901 Ch.5 Tristates), yosys does not.

What is the fix?

assign bus = sel ? b : a;
