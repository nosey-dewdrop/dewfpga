Why does it happen?

yosys' own reader has no enum methods; yosys-slang implements first() and last() and stops at next()/prev(). A known gap of the chain.

What is the fix?

case (s) IDLE: s <= RUN; RUN: s <= DONE; DONE: s <= IDLE; endcase
